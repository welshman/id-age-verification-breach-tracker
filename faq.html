FAQ, methodology & ethics

What this site is

What does this site do?

It tracks publicly reported data breaches involving identity documents and the companies that process them for identity verification, KYC, or age verification.

What this site is not
  • It is not a way to check whether your specific passport or ID number has leaked.
  • It is not affiliated with any vendor, government, or Have I Been Pwned.
  • It is not a complete record of every breach.

How data is collected and updated

Where does the breach data come from?

Entries are based on public reporting, security research, regulatory findings, and direct company notices. Each entry links to its original sources.

How is the site updated?

A scheduled GitHub Actions workflow scans configured RSS feeds and public sources for candidate incidents. New claims require review before publication.

Privacy & ethics

Do you store personal data?

No. This static site reads pre-generated public data files only and never requests actual ID or passport numbers.

Limitations

What should I keep in mind?
  • Many breaches are never publicly disclosed.
  • Some attribution is disputed and is labelled as such.
  • Some entries document age-verification control failures rather than data leaks.
  • Inclusion of a company does not imply wrongdoing.
